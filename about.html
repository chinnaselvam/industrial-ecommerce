About Stackly

Our Story

Powering industrial confidence for over a decade.

Stackly was founded to make high-quality industrial tools and machinery more accessible to businesses that rely on productivity, safety, and long-term durability. From manufacturing units to contractors and service workshops, we help teams get the right tools for the job.

Our focus is simple: accurate product selection, dependable after-sales support, and premium-grade equipment that performs in demanding conditions.
